Research

Question, Test, and Explain.

My master’s thesis, with code and notebooks on GitHub.

MSc thesis, Gisma University of Applied Sciences, winter 2026. Supervisor: Prof. William Morrison.

Cross-lingual and few-shot offensive language detection in low-resource Dravidian languages

Social platforms need to moderate comments in languages that have almost no labelled data. I compared two cheap ways to get there: fine-tuning the multilingual transformer XLM-R with 0 to 500 target-language examples, and prompting the small LLM Qwen2.5-1.5B with in-context examples. I evaluated on code-mixed Kannada, Tamil and Malayalam, with Macro-F1, LIME explanations and three random seeds.

0.61

Macro-F1 on Kannada with zero Kannada labels, transferring from English, German and Hindi.

6 of 6

Language and shot settings where fine-tuned XLM-R beat in-context learning with Qwen2.5.

~90%

Of the full-data baseline reached with only 500 labels for Kannada and Tamil.

96%

Accuracy from a Malayalam model that predicted one class. Macro-F1 exposed it at 0.49.

What I’d tell a team

  • Report Macro-F1, not accuracy, when classes are imbalanced.
  • Run several seeds before trusting a few-shot result.
  • Try a class-weighted simple baseline before a large model.
